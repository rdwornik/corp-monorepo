Usage: corp ingest-extractions [OPTIONS] CKE_OUTPUT_PATH

  Ingest CKE extraction output into the vault.

  Reads CKE output packages, validates frontmatter, applies quality gate, and
  copies accepted notes to vault/01_Knowledge/ (flat). Cover slides go to
  _assets/. Failed notes go to _quarantine/. Respects trust_level=verified.

  Examples:

      corp ingest-extractions /path/to/cke/output

      corp ingest-extractions /path/to/cke/output --dry-run

      corp ingest-extractions /path/to/cke/output --rebuild-index

      corp ingest-extractions /path/to/cke/output --quality-threshold 50

Options:
  --dry-run                    Show what would be ingested
  --force                      Overwrite even verified notes
  --quality-threshold INTEGER  Min quality_score to accept (0-100)
  --rebuild-index              Rebuild FTS5 search index after ingest
  --help                       Show this message and exit.
